Seven years below

Our story

Five small chapters.
One long yes.

It began in a classroom. It ends — begins, really — at a mandapam in Rajahmundry.

One

The First Class

One classroom. One list. One day.
Two names, one seat away.
No plan, no promise, nothing said —
and still, a story lay ahead.

Two

A Quiet Yes

No grand speech. No perfect line.
Just days that taught us what we knew:
that "friend" had grown too small a word
for everything we'd grown into.

Three

The Years Between

New cities came. New work. New rooms.
The map grew wide, the years grew fast.
But every evening, one small call
kept seven years from slipping past.

Four

Two Families

Two families met. The elders spoke.
Much tea was poured, much laughter too.
And somewhere in that afternoon,
one simple yes made all things true.

Five

Six Past Eleven

The pandit read the almanac
and gave us one bright square of time:
the twenty-seventh, six past eleven.
Come bless us. Come and make it rhyme.
