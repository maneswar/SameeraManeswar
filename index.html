Our story

Four small chapters.
One long yes.

It began in an MCA classroom. Seven years later, it's leading to a mandapam in Rajahmundry.

One

Two Strangers, One Classroom

Two strangers walked into the very same class,
not knowing this hello would forever last.
Notes were shared, and so were the jokes,
somewhere in that room, a friendship softly woke.

Two

Best Friends First

Assignments, exams, and canteen chai,
somehow together, the days just flew by.
From project partners to partners in crime,
we built a friendship, one laugh at a time.

Three

Falling, Slowly and Sweetly

Somewhere between the notes and the noise,
a feeling grew, quiet but full of poise.
A friend became someone we couldn't miss,
and just like that, our story turned to this.

Four

Seven Years, One Forever

Seven years of friendship, love and laughter loud,
of standing by each other, feeling so proud.
Now the classroom sweethearts finally say "I do",
here's to forever — me, and you.
